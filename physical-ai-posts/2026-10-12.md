# Physical AI – 3 post ideas (week of Oct 8–15, 2026)

Focus: technical breakthroughs and real field deployments. Funding news excluded.

**Honest note:** This week had no major new technical breakthrough. Most items were
analysis pieces on reliability. The strongest hard numbers are older, so every source
carries its date. Figures are company-reported unless stated.

---

## Idea 1 – "Dexterity isn't the bottleneck. Reliability is."
**Angle:** Demos show capability, but 50% -> 70% success still means "doesn't work".
Humanoid is now targeting 99.9% manipulation reliability. The metric to watch is
success rate over thousands of hours, not demo videos.

**Sources**
- MIT Technology Review, "AI breakthroughs in robotics won't change your life any time soon" – date: 2026-10-08. Raibert: 50->70% still "doesn't work"; Hurst: more data alone is a "fundamentally flawed premise". https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/08/1145923/ai-breakthroughs-in-robotics-wont-change-your-life-any-time-soon/
- The Robot Report, "Why dexterity is physical AI's real bottleneck" – date: 2026-10-09. https://www.therobotreport.com/why-dexterity-is-physical-ai-real-bottleneck/

## Idea 2 – "One checkpoint, new robot, <200 demos": cross-embodiment is getting practical
**Angle:** Gemini Robotics On-Device 2 reportedly adapts to new bi-arm robots in hours
with fewer than 200 examples (SO101 success 6.7% -> 53.3%, Dexmate 33.3% -> 75.6%, per a
secondary source, unverified). Caveats: DeepMind's own benchmarks, "example" undefined,
early access only. Contrast with MimicX-style work on physics-feasible motion references.

**Sources**
- Google DeepMind blog, Gemini Robotics 2 – date: 2026-07-30 (OLD, ~11 weeks). https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/
- arXiv:2610.09055 MimicX (policy-in-the-loop refinement of video-derived humanoid motion) – date: ~2026-10-08, preprint, not peer reviewed.
- Nature Will Natura-1 (dexterous-hand foundation model, 20 DoF) – date: 2026-10-13, secondary source only, claims unverified.

## Idea 3 – "Boring robots in real workflows": where physical AI is actually running
**Angle:** Real deployments are narrow: bins/totes, inspection, companionship, cargo
drones. Form follows workflow. Field numbers: Siemens Erlangen trial 60 container
moves/h, >90% pick success; Xiaomi nut-tightening 90.2% -> 98%; Spot inspection at a
Michelin plant; NYSOFA ElliQ rollout ($2.1M, 3 years); AWS open-source physical-AI
toolchain.
