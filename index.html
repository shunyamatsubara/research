Question Ground Reasoning So-what
A short course in checking your own thinking

Weakest Link

A chain of thinking is only as strong as its weakest link. In this course you build your own checklist for finding that link, and a way to fix it in a back-and-forth with AI.

Why this course

You learn new fields fast and you write to explain and argue. The problems you named: too much input, hidden assumptions found too late, and analysis that ends without a clear "so what". Each lesson fixes one of these with one small skill, and adds one line to your checklist.

Full mission: MISSION.md in your course folder.
