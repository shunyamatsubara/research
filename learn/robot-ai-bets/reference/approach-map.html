Approach map

Place any robot-AI company with two questions

Q1 · Where does the general knowledge come from?

Web text + images · web video · human video and wearables · simulation.
(Almost everyone also finishes with some real robot data.)

Q2 · What does the model predict when it runs?

Actions directly · the future (video or features), then actions · parts (points, grasps, plans) for a classical planner.

Three designs of world action model

  1. Inverse dynamics: generate future video, then a separate model infers actions — Rhoda DVA, 1X, UniPi, mimic-video, LingBot-VA.
  2. Joint: video and actions from one model — DreamZero, Cosmos Policy, GR-1/GR-2.
  3. Representation-only: trained with video, generates none at run time — Fast-WAM.

Head-to-head numbers worth remembering

  • Cosmos Policy vs π0.5, LIBERO: 98.5 vs 96.9. [1]
  • DreamZero vs π0.5, unseen DROID tasks: 22.5% vs 7.5%. [2]
  • π0.5 beats WAMs on LIBERO-Plus robustness: 85.7 vs 82.2. [3]
  • Inference time: π0.5 63 ms vs WAMs 190–1,175 ms. [3]
  • Modular TiPToP (0 h robot data) vs π0.5-DROID (350 h): 98 vs 55 of 165. [4]
  • Rhoda vs anyone: not published.

Quick placement checklist

  1. What is pretrained, on what data, how many hours? → Q1.
  2. What comes out of the model at run time: actions, video, features, or points/grasps? → Q2.
  3. How many hours of robot data per new task and per new robot type?
  4. How fast does it run, on which GPU, on or off the robot?
  5. What evidence: own demos, public benchmark, or third-party test?

Trend to watch: the families are merging (π0.7 uses subgoal images from a “lightweight world model”; NVIDIA's GR00T N2 is a world action model; NVIDIA expects “WAM+VLA hybrids”). Labels mean less every month; the two questions still work. Lessons: 1 · Rhoda's bet · 2 · The contenders.
