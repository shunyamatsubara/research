# Starting knowledge: hands-on with VLAs, strong on classical manipulation

The learner has fine-tuned and run a VLA model, and knows forward/inverse kinematics and motion planning (see the earlier article in `learn/slides/20261010-pixel-to-grasp-pose/`). So lessons can assume what a VLA is, what action chunks and teleoperation data are, and how a modular pick-and-place pipeline works, and should spend their effort on the *differences* between approaches and on how to judge a company's claims.

**Evidence**: stated by the learner on 2026-10-17 ("I have used one"); the earlier article's brief.

**Implications**: Do not re-teach VLA basics. Camera geometry and image processing are weaker areas, so explain any pixel-level idea (for example, why predicting pixels is expensive) from first principles.
