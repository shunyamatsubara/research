# Mission: Robot foundation-model bets (starting with Rhoda AI)

## Why
I advise clients on robot AI and help them choose partners and vendors. I need to explain what each robot-AI company is betting on, judge whether the bet is sound, and compare it with the approaches that compete with it — at an engineer's level of detail, not a press-release level.

## Success looks like
- Explain Rhoda AI's Direct Video Action approach to a client in about two minutes: what goes in, what comes out, and where robot data is still needed.
- Name the core hypotheses behind a company's approach, and say what evidence would confirm or break each one.
- Place any new robot-AI company on the approach map (VLA, video / world-action model, human video, real-robot data scaling, simulation, modular pipeline) within minutes of reading its announcement.
- Write a due-diligence question list for a partner or vendor meeting.
- Recommend which approach fits a given client use case (for example, factory pick-and-place), with reasons.

## Constraints
- Engineer level: architecture, data amounts, control rates and benchmark numbers are welcome.
- English, in plain words (the learner is a non-native speaker).
- The learner has fine-tuned and run VLA models, and knows forward/inverse kinematics and motion planning.
- Lessons must be short; one clear win per lesson.

## Out of scope
- Training or implementing these models (for now).
- Company valuation and finance, except where money signals the bet (for example, building their own hardware).
