Robot Startup Moats

A short course for one engineer: learn to judge a robot startup's technical claims, and to tell a real moat from a public recipe.

Mission

Judge robotics startups and their claims, for example whether a company's "locomotion model" is a real moat. Every lesson is chosen to serve this.

Lessons

  1. 0001 Is locomotion a commodity?

    Split "locomotion" into four layers, then test your two arguments: the simple reward and the easy simulation. About 12 minutes.

Reference sheets

  1. R1 The locomotion stack

    The four layers, their status in 2026, and the questions to ask any startup. Made to print on one page.

How this course works

  • Each lesson teaches one thing, then makes you practice it. Only your first answer counts.
  • The reference sheets keep the short version. Look at them again a few days after each lesson.
  • Ask your teacher (Claude, in the chat) about anything that is unclear. Report your score and your answers there too: the next lesson is planned from them.

Source files live in learn/teach/robot-moats/ in the research repository.
