# Course re-planned around client themes, split into "extension" and "new"

On 2026-10-16 the learner listed seven themes they need for client work: AI decision and motion-range control (including the permissions of autonomous AI), protection of training data, models and manufacturing know-how, configuration management of AI models and OSS, remote operation and cloud security, camera data handling, how physical-AI risks differ from conventional robots, and building safety and security in from PoC to production. They framed the work as separating what is an extension of conventional measures from what needs new measures. The course was re-planned around these themes (see NOTES.md); MISSION.md is unchanged until the learner says whether the themes come from a specific client project.

**Evidence**: the learner's own message of 2026-10-16, and "OK" to the proposed re-plan.

**Implications**: Every lesson ends with an "extension / new" table, and practice includes sorting controls into the two groups. Lessons 4 and 5 need new research on confidentiality (trade secrets, data contracts) and on camera-data privacy before they can be written.
