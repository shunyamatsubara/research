## Knowledge

### LNG basics and pricing
- [EIA: "Liquefied natural gas explained" (updated 21 Jun 2024)](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/natural-gas/liquefied-natural-gas.php)
  US government basics: −162 °C, 600× smaller, carriers, regasification. Use for: first explanations of the chain. Primary source for Lesson 1.
- [EIA Today in Energy: "Ten years after first Sabine Pass cargo, U.S. LNG exports are still on the rise" (24 Feb 2026)](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67224)
  How US LNG contracts are priced (115% × Henry Hub + inflation-linked liquefaction fee; oil-linked at 12–13% of Brent); US exports 15.0 Bcf/d in 2025. Use for: the cost side of the spread.
- [S&P Global Platts: "Platts changes JKM LNG cargo quantity standard specification" (Feb 2026)](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/pricing-benchmarks/our-methodology/subscriber-notes/021626-platts-changes-jkm-lng-cargo-quantity-standard-specification)
  JKM standard cargo 3.3–3.5 TBtu (was 3.4); JKM basis is delivered ex-ship to Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan. Use for: the "$1 ≈ $3.4 million per cargo" rule. (Page blocks automated reading; facts from search summaries.)
- [Canada Energy Regulator: conversion tables](https://apps.cer-rec.gc.ca/Conversion/conversion-tables.aspx) and [OAPEC conversion table](https://www.oapecorg.org/Frontend/flippingbook/OAPEC/AnnualReports/AnnualReport2013/files/assets/basic-html/page161.html)
  1 Mt LNG ≈ 48.03 Bcf ≈ 48.57 TBtu. Use for: Mt/yr → cargoes a year (≈ 14).
