# Diamond Global Energy (DGE) Resources

## Knowledge

### LNG basics and pricing
- [EIA: "Liquefied natural gas explained" (updated 21 Jun 2024)](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/natural-gas/liquefied-natural-gas.php)
  US government basics: −162 °C, 600× smaller, carriers, regasification. Use for: first explanations of the chain.
- [EIA Today in Energy: "Ten years after first Sabine Pass cargo, U.S. LNG exports are still on the rise" (24 Feb 2026)](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67224)
  How US LNG contracts are priced (115% × Henry Hub + inflation-linked liquefaction fee; oil-linked at 12–13% of Brent), US export growth (15.0 Bcf/d in 2025, >18.1 Bcf/d forecast for 2027). Use for: the cost side of the spread.
- [S&P Global Platts: "Platts changes JKM LNG cargo quantity standard specification" (Feb 2026)](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/pricing-benchmarks/our-methodology/subscriber-notes/021626-platts-changes-jkm-lng-cargo-quantity-standard-specification)
  JKM standard cargo is 3.3–3.5 TBtu (was 3.4 TBtu). JKM basis: delivered ex-ship to Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan. Use for: cargo size and the "$1 ≈ $3.4 million per cargo" rule. (Page blocks automated reading; facts taken from search summaries of the note.)
- [Canada Energy Regulator: energy conversion tables](https://apps.cer-rec.gc.ca/Conversion/conversion-tables.aspx)
  1 million tonnes LNG ≈ 48.03 Bcf of gas. Use for: unit conversions.
- [OAPEC Annual Report 2013, conversion table (p.161)](https://www.oapecorg.org/Frontend/flippingbook/OAPEC/AnnualReports/AnnualReport2013/files/assets/basic-html/page161.html)
  1 million tonnes LNG ≈ 48.57 TBtu. Use for: mtpa → cargoes per year (≈ 14).
- [Argus: "US LNG margins tighten on higher Henry Hub prices" (Dec 2025)](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2762492-us-lng-margins-tighten-on-higher-henry-hub-prices)
  Indicative US long-term price of 115% HH + $3/MMBtu; the fixed fee is paid whether or not LNG is taken. Use for: why a thin spread hurts. (Page did not load for automated reading; quoted via search summary. Re-check by hand if it matters.)
