Four impact channels

ChannelWhat changesWhere on the chainTypical events
PriceSale price falls or gas cost rises, so the spread shrinksGas field, buyerNew supply, mild weather, US gas price jump
VolumeFewer cargoes to sell, or fewer buyers for themGas field, plant, buyerPlant outage, pipeline problem, tariff, demand drop
ShippingVoyage is longer, slower, dearer, or blockedShipCanal limits, war risk at sea, storms, ship shortage
CreditThe other side does not pay, or does not do what the contract saysBuyerLate payment, bankruptcy, sanctions on a buyer

Events are causes; channels are effects. Tag every event with its channel and its place on the chain.

Price names

Henry Hub
The main US natural gas price, set at a pipeline hub in Louisiana. US LNG costs follow it.
JKM (Japan Korea Marker)
The main spot price for LNG delivered to Japan, South Korea, China and Taiwan, published by S&P Global Platts.
Brent
A world oil price. Many long-term LNG contracts in Asia are priced as a percentage of it.
