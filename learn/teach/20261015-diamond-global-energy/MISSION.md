# Mission: Know Diamond Global Energy well enough to build its risk-monitoring system

## Why
MCD3 is already running a project with Diamond Global Energy (DGE), Mitsubishi Corporation's LNG marketing and trading company in Singapore. Phase 1 is market and geopolitical monitoring. The next phases are early risk-signal detection and credit risk management. As the engineer and data scientist on the project, the learner must know which events matter to DGE and why, so the system watches the right things and ranks its alerts well.

## Success looks like
- Explain in three sentences what DGE does, where its LNG comes from, and how it makes money.
- Take any news headline and say which impact channel it hits for DGE (price, volume, shipping or credit) and why.
- Name DGE's biggest pressures in 2026, each backed by a source.
- Explain how credit risk arises when DGE sells LNG, and list early signals that a buyer may not pay or perform.
- Ask DGE staff questions that improve the system design: what to watch, which thresholds, which alerts come first.

## Constraints
- About one week (target: 2026-10-22). Three short lessons.
- New to LNG. Strong engineering and data background.
- Plain English (the learner is a non-native speaker).
- Public sources only. Statements about DGE's internal work are hypotheses to check with DGE.

## Out of scope
- LNG plant engineering and ship technology.
- Writing the monitoring system's code. This course gives the domain knowledge for it.
- Mitsubishi Corporation businesses outside LNG.
