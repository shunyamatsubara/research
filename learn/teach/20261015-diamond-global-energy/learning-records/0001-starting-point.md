# Starting point: new to LNG, strong in engineering and data

On 2026-10-15 the learner said they are new to LNG: they do not yet know how LNG is produced, shipped, priced or traded. They are the engineer / data scientist on MCD3's running project with DGE (phase 1: market and geopolitical monitoring; later: early risk-signal detection and credit risk management).

**Implications**: Teach the LNG chain from zero, but do not explain data or software ideas. Use engineering analogies freely (flows, pipelines, signals, thresholds). Every business idea should end in something the monitoring system could watch.
