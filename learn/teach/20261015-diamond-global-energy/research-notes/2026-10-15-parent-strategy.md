# Mitsubishi Corporation's LNG strategy and DGE's place in it (research digest, 2026-10-15)

## Fact sheet

### DGE identity and contracts
- **DGE is the renamed Diamond Gas International Pte. Ltd. (DGI).** Same UEN 201323623E; GLEIF lists DGI as "previous legal name" (https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/254900YEU05SFCYLUW25, updated 2026-07-01) — high. Everything MC has said about "DGI" applies to DGE.
- **Trading windows.** A Platts note on the rename says DGE asked to join Platts price-assessment windows for APAC physical, APAC paper and Atlantic physical LNG (https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/pricing-benchmarks/our-methodology/subscriber-notes/041526-diamond-gas-international-pte-ltd-changes-entity-name-to-diamond-global-energy-pte-ltd, 2026-04-15) — medium (extract).
- **Remit (Tokyo Gas, 2018):** "(1) marketing of LNG produced by projects in the U.S. and Canada in which Mitsubishi Corporation has equities, (2) new business development in emerging LNG markets, and (3) LNG short-term trading and optimization". Set up 2013, 100% MC-owned (https://www.tokyo-gas.co.jp/Press_e/20181010-01e.pdf) — high.
- **CEO and ships.** CEO Tetsuro Wada. DIAMOND GAS NEXUS on long-term charter to DGE, mainly for Cameron LNG (https://www.nyk.com/english/news/2026/20260925_03.html, 2026-09-25) — high.
- **Cameron contract chain.** MC Global Gas Corp. holds MC's Cameron capacity and sells to DGI under a firm 20-year FOB contract dated 2019-06-17; volume is a pro-rata share of 209.3m MMBtu/yr, about 4 Mtpa (https://www.energy.gov/documents/diamond-gas-international-pte-ltdpdf) — high.
- **Long-term sales.** All delivered ex-ship (DES), so DGI arranges shipping. LNG Canada deals are HOAs; conversion to binding contracts not verified.
  - JERA: up to ≈1.2 Mtpa from LNG Canada, ≈15 years from FY2024 (https://www.jera.co.jp/en/news/information/20190409_338, 2019-04-09)
  - Tokyo Gas: up to 0.6 Mtpa from LNG Canada for 2026–2039, plus ≈0.2 Mtpa from Cameron (Tokyo Gas link above)
  - Toho Gas: ≈0.3 Mtpa from LNG Canada for 15 years (https://www.tohogas.co.jp/lang-n/en/corporate/press/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2018/10/10/20181010_1.pdf)
- **Bought-in supply.** Santos sells DGI 1.5 Mtpa of Barossa LNG for 10 years, priced off JKM (https://www.offshore-technology.com/news/santos-mitsubishi-sign-long-term-lng-supply-agreement/, Dec 2020) — high.

### Corporate Strategy 2027 (CS2027)
- **Targets** (announced 2025-04-03; https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/mitsubishi-unveils-corporate-strategy-2027-for-growth-93CH-3964694 and https://www.bairdmaritime.com/shipping/ports/ceo-says-mitsubishi-may-consider-investing-in-alaska-lng-project) — high:
  - ROE of 12% or more by FY2027; underlying operating cash flow growth averaging 10% or more.
  - About ¥1tn of sustaining investment plus more than ¥3tn of growth investment; net debt-to-equity capped at about 0.6x.
  - Reuters reported a ¥1.2tn net-profit goal.
- **Investment raised** to more than ¥1.3tn sustaining plus more than ¥3.3tn growth (https://www.mitsubishicorp.com/jp/en/ir/library/meetings/pdf/260501/20260501e.pdf, 2026-05-01) — medium (extract).
- **LNG.** Gas framed as a "pragmatic low-carbon solution". Equity LNG capacity from ≈15 to ≈18 Mtpa by the early 2030s; equity LNG was 14.90 Mtpa in July 2025 (JOGMEC: https://journal.jogmec.go.jp/content/300799935.pdf; Reuters: https://boereport.com/2025/06/30/mitsubishi-aims-to-boost-its-equity-lng-production-to-18-million-tons-year-by-early-2030s/). No explicit LNG-trading target found.

### Earnings and IR
- **FY2025 / FY2026 forecast.** FY2025 net income ¥800.5bn. FY2026 forecast ¥1.1tn, citing US shale, "stable operations at LNG Canada" and asset-sale gains; includes a ¥30bn contingency for a Hormuz closure (20260501e above) — medium (extract).
- **Old Environmental Energy segment.** Net income fell from ¥198.6bn to ¥160.9bn in FY2025, partly on start-up costs in "North American LNG / Equity LNG marketing" (https://edinetdb.com/company/E02529/text) — medium.
- **Q1 FY2026** (2026-08-03; https://www.mitsubishicorp.com/jp/en/ir/library/meetings/pdf/260803/20260803e.pdf) — medium (extract):
  - Group net income ¥298.5bn.
  - Energy & Power Solution segment OCF +¥51.4bn (+85%), credited to the start-up of "North American LNG / Equity LNG marketing", which "benefited from a concentration of spot sales in Q1".
  - LNG Canada passed its 100th cargo; profit contribution starts in FY2026, full from FY2027.
- **Risks** (edinetdb) — medium:
  - MC's LNG sold mostly under long-term oil-linked contracts; $1/bbl oil ≈ ¥2.4bn annual net income.
  - Spot LNG moved from $9–14/MMBtu to over $20 in March 2026 on Hormuz fears and damage to Qatari facilities; MC expects prices to "remain highly volatile".

### US
- **Cameron LNG (12 Mtpa).** MC holds 16.6% via Japan LNG Investment (MC 70%, NYK 30%) and ≈4 Mtpa of tolling capacity for 2019–2039 (JOGMEC) — high.
- **Cameron Phase 2** (Sempra 10-Q filed 2026-08-06: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001032208/000103220826000045/sre-20260630.htm) — high: Train 4 (≈6.75 Mtpa) has no FID, only a non-binding HOA; Sempra Infrastructure would take 50.2%; the rest split equally among Phase-1 customers including MC; permits run to March 2033.
- **Aethon / Haynesville** — high: agreed 2026-01-16, closed mid-July 2026; ≈$5.2bn equity + ≈$2.33bn debt, ≈$7.5bn total; ≈2.1 Bcf/d (≈15 Mtpa LNG-equivalent); MC said it will use "Diamond Gas International" for a "Gas to LNG initiative" (https://www.mitsubishicorp.com/jp/en/news/release/2026/20260116002.html; https://www.mitsubishicorp.com/jp/en/news/release/2026/20260715001.html; https://fortune.com/2026/07/14/mitsubishi-takes-over-7billion-gas-fields-aethon-japanese-push-lng-ai/).
- **New US offtake agreements, 2023–2026:** none found. Alaska LNG: MC "considering" only — medium.

### Canada
- **LNG Canada (14 Mtpa).** MC 15% via Diamond LNG Canada; each partner lifts its own share; DGI markets MC's ≈2.1 Mtpa, mainly to Japan; first cargo 2025-06-30 (https://en.acnnewswire.com/press-release/english/100619/first-shipment-of-lng-from-the-lng-canada-project) — high.
- **Ramp-up problems:** Train 1 ≈50% in July 2025 (S&P, medium); Shell targeted full capacity in 2026 (https://www.worldoil.com/news/2025/10/6/shell-s-lng-canada-project-nears-first-train-2-cargo-amid-production-ramp-up/); Train 2 down in December 2025 (Reuters); unplanned flaring from 2026-03-11 (https://pgjonline.com/news/2026/march/shell-led-lng-canada-warns-flaring-at-kitimat-export-terminal-could-last-a-week); in February 2026 MC said full-capacity operation through FY2026 was uncertain (medium).
- **Possible stake sale:** Reuters (2026-01-16) reported MC hired RBC to explore options for its 15% (https://boereport.com/2026/01/16/shell-mitsubishi-exploring-sale-options-for-their-stakes-in-lng-canada-sources-say/).
- **Phase 2 FID** (2026-09-28/29): capacity to 28 Mtpa from the early 2030s; MC invests ≈¥500bn; MC offtake from 2.1 to 4.2 Mtpa, mainly for Japan (newswire link above; https://finance.biggo.com/news/ace4edf1-0ec7-4209-84d4-3d8fd0ebb755; https://www.energyflux.news/japans-lng-love-story-is-not-over-yet/) — medium-high.

## How DGE fits ([V] verified by the agent, [I] inferred)
1. **Gas [V].** US: Adamas produces Haynesville gas (not yet tied to LNG); CIMA buys feed gas. Canada: Diamond LNG Canada buys Montney gas.
2. **Liquefaction [V].** Cameron: MC Global Gas pays a tolling fee, keeps title to the LNG and sells ≈4 Mtpa FOB to DGE. LNG Canada: MC lifts ≈2.1 Mtpa, which DGE markets.
3. **What DGE does [V].** Long-term delivered sales to JERA, Tokyo Gas and Toho Gas (up to ≈2.1 Mtpa); charters ships; buys Barossa LNG on JKM pricing; seeks to trade physical and paper LNG in Asia and the Atlantic.
4. **Scale [I].** Disclosed supply ≈7.6 Mtpa. MC's Asia-Pacific equity LNG is probably sold by the projects themselves.
5. **Flexibility [I].** Mainly from Cameron FOB cargoes and spot trading; LNG Canada volume looks largely pre-sold.
6. **Mandate [I].** The "Global Energy" rename and the "Gas to LNG" plan suggest a wider remit; nothing announced.

## Parent pressures likely to flow down to DGE
1. **Earnings.** CS2027 and the ¥1.1tn FY2026 forecast lean partly on LNG Canada; "North American LNG / Equity LNG marketing" is a named driver; spot sales lifted Q1. DGE's swings are visible to investors.
2. **Volumes to place.** Another 2.1 Mtpa from LNG Canada Phase 2; growth toward the 18 Mtpa target; possible Haynesville gas-to-LNG volumes; ≈1.1 Mtpa from Cameron Train 4 if it proceeds (agent's calculation) — landing in a market DGE's then-CEO expected to be oversupplied from 2027–28.
3. **Delivery risk [I].** Delivered commitments plus uncertain ramp-up leave DGE with shipping and cover-purchase risk.
4. **Price and risk discipline.** Barossa is JKM-priced while MC's book is mostly oil-linked (index mismatch [I]); ¥30bn Hormuz contingency; DGE's risk limits are not public.
5. **Capital discipline.** No "indiscriminate" LNG expansion; leverage capped; stake sale explored. DGE's ability to sell profitably likely feeds those decisions [I].
6. **Two-basin role.** "Eastward and westward flows", Eneco and "regional and product diversification" point to DGE covering both the Atlantic and the Pacific.
7. **Transition and AI [I, low].** MC-wide pushes; no evidence at DGE.
