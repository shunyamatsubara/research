## Pain points for Diamond Global Energy (DGE), LNG marketer, as of 15 Oct 2026

**Bottom line:** The LNG glut expected for 2026 did not happen, because of the Iran war.
- The Strait of Hormuz carried about 20% of global LNG and has been largely shut since the end of February 2026.
- North American sellers currently enjoy a very wide price spread. In Q2-2026 Henry Hub averaged $2.95/MMBtu against $17.5 for Asian spot LNG (IEA Q3-2026, 7 Jul 2026: https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/72079a0a-ec1e-48a1-b8e9-3042160b9378/GasMarketReport,Q3-2026.pdf).
- So DGE's pains are mostly volatility, competition, credit and timing, not low prices today. The glut is postponed to about 2029, not cancelled.

### Pain points

**1. The oversupply has moved into DGE's 2028–30 selling window**
- Evidence:
  - Before the war, the IEA expected 2026 LNG supply to grow 7% (more than 40 bcm), over 85% of it from North America (IEA Q1-2026, Jan 2026: https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/f746c0aa-03f3-47ba-a0d9-b45c3c758150/GasMarketReport,Q1-2026.pdf).
  - The IEA now estimates war-related losses of about 140 bcm over 2026–30. That is only 15% of the new supply due in that period, and it is concentrated in 2026–27 (IEA Q3).
  - GlobalData sees the surplus peaking at 199 bcm in 2029 (14 Oct 2026: https://www.offshore-technology.com/analyst-comment/gas-shock-delays-lng-glut-export-routes/). BNEF sees more than 100 Mt of oversupply by 2031 (14 Jul 2026: https://www.semafor.com/article/07/14/2026/global-market-faces-lng-glut-while-us-forecasts-shortfall).
  - Platts JKM forward prices on 25 Mar 2026: $15.13 for 2027, $11.13 for 2028, $9.08 for 2029. A new US FOB contract (119% of Henry Hub plus a $2.80–3.20 fee) costs $7.39–7.77 before shipping (28 Mar 2026: https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/venture-global-vitol-lng-deal-targets-upside-in-2026-27-spot-prices/).
  - Goldman expects about four months of US cargo cancellations in each of 2028 and 2029. Poten expects about 100 cancelled cargoes in 2029–30 (19 Dec 2025: https://gasoutlook.com/analysis/u-s-lng-profitability-window-temporary-closed/).
- Why it hurts DGE: Mitsubishi's Cameron LNG tolling fee is fixed. On the March forward prices, the margin left after buying US FOB supply roughly equals the cost of shipping by 2029 (agent's arithmetic). LNG Canada Phase 2 volume arrives around the same time.
- Severity: **High.** It sets the value of term sales and of the tolling commitment.

**2. Extreme price volatility**
- Evidence:
  - TTF month-ahead volatility reached about 170% in March 2026. JKM fell 12% between 15 and 26 June after the US–Iran interim deal (IEA Q3).
  - Asian spot jumped from $16.81 to $22 in one week in July (Kpler, 24 Jul 2026: https://www.kpler.com/blog/global-lng-and-natural-gas-prices-surge-as-us-and-iran-resume-hot-war).
  - It hit $27 on 20 Sep, a 45-month high (https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/20/09/2026/asia-spot-lng-prices-hit-fresh-45-month-high).
  - JKM was $26.39 on 14 Oct 2026, up 140% year on year (https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/liquefied-natural-gas-japan-korea).
- Why it hurts DGE: its costs are Henry Hub-linked while its sales are JKM/TTF-linked, so the basis moves fast. It also faces collateral calls on hedges and must re-allocate cargoes within days.
- Severity: **High.** This affects every hedge and cargo decision in 2026.

**3. Asian buyers cut purchases when prices are high**
- Evidence:
  - The IEA cut its 2026 Asian gas demand forecast from +4% to −0.5%. India is forecast at −8%, Japan about −5%, Korea about −1%. China's LNG imports fell 12% and Pakistan's 70% in March–June (IEA Q3).
  - China's LNG imports had already fallen 14% in 2025 (IEA Q1).
  - PetroChina says demand normalises only below $10 (15 Sep 2026: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/lng-demand-china-india-could-083000371.html).
  - Pakistan rejected BP Singapore's $26.969 bid (3 Sep 2026: https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/09/03/pakistan-lng-rejects-dollar2697mmbtu-spot-cargo-bid-retenders-for-september).
  - Bangladesh is paying $28.95–29.80 for October cargoes (Daily Star, 15 Sep 2026: https://asianews.network/?p=301070).
- Why it hurts DGE: South and Southeast Asia is the growth outlet for flexible North American LNG. Those buyers cancel tenders or switch to coal at high prices, and come back only when margins are thin.
- Severity: **Medium-High.**

**4. Too many portfolio sellers, and buyers going direct on shorter terms**
- Evidence:
  - In 2025, portfolio players bought 57% of all contracted LNG and 90% of destination-flexible volume. The correlation between Asian and European prices hit a record 0.955, which leaves less arbitrage (IEA Q1).
  - Japanese companies resold 40% of the LNG they handled in FY2024, up from 16% in FY2018 (IEEFA, reported 17 Mar 2026: https://greentechlead.com/renewable-energy/japan-transforms-into-global-lng-trading-hub-as-resales-hit-record-40-amid-shifting-energy-demand-52649).
  - Chinese buyers hold about 14 Mtpa of US contracts and resell those cargoes. Long-term US supply delivers to Northeast Asia at about $8, roughly a third of spot (Bloomberg, 21 Sep 2026: https://www.energyconnects.com/news/gas-lng/2026/september/trump-xi-talks-could-mean-6-billion-of-us-natural-gas-for-china/).
  - 24.5 Mt of US capacity under construction has no long-term buyer (Reuters, 18 Sep 2026: https://boereport.com/2026/09/18/us-china-discuss-cutting-tariffs-on-us-lng-ahead-of-xi-visit/).
  - The US has more than 20 Bcf/d of export capacity, with over 38 Bcf/d operating or under construction (DOE, 30 Sep 2026: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-09/LNG%20Snapshot%20September%2030%202026.pdf).
  - Government-brokered deals push buyers straight to US projects. Example: Hanwha signed with Venture Global for 1.5 Mtpa (13 Mar 2026: https://www.energyconnects.com/news/gas-lng/2026/march/asia-set-to-pledge-30-billion-in-energy-mineral-deals-with-us/).
  - Contracting slowed: 25 deals (26 Mtpa) in January–May 2026 versus about 70 deals in 2025 (Poten, 8 Jul 2026: https://gasoutlook.com/analysis/iran-war-delays-but-does-not-end-looming-lng-glut/). Buyers are shifting to shorter, more flexible deals (Energy Intelligence, 22 Sep 2026: https://www.energyintel.com/000001a0-c82c-dda7-adb0-f96fdc100000).
- Why it hurts DGE: its Henry Hub-linked cargoes are a commodity that majors, traders, JERA, Tokyo Gas and Chinese state companies also sell.
- Severity: **High.**

**5. China is closed to US cargoes**
- Evidence:
  - China has charged a 15% tariff on US LNG since February 2025. US LNG ships to China fell from 64 in 2024 to effectively zero in 2025 (Reuters, 18 Sep 2026).
  - US LNG was left off China's provisional tariff-cut list after Xi's US visit (29 Sep 2026: https://www.energyintel.com/000001a0-e78a-d3a5-aff8-efaf07830000).
- Why it hurts DGE: Cameron cargoes are shut out of the largest importer. Portfolio planning has to keep US-origin and Canadian-origin cargoes separate.
- Severity: **Medium.**

**6. Feed-gas cost could rise just as selling prices fall**
- Evidence:
  - During Winter Storm Fern, Henry Hub spot went above $30 and US feed gas to LNG plants fell 40% on 22–26 Jan (28 Jan 2026: https://globallnghub.com/henry-hub-gas-prices-surge-as-storm-fern-triggers-freeze-offs-and-power-demand-spike/).
  - The EIA reports a January 2026 average of $7.72 and forecasts $3.48 for 2026 and $3.16 for 2027 (6 Oct 2026: https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/natgas.php).
  - BNEF expects a US gas supply deficit by 2028 (14 Jul 2026, link above).
  - Goldman says late-2020s Henry Hub futures sit below the cost of producing in the weaker parts of the Haynesville (Dec 2025, link above).
- Why it hurts DGE: Henry Hub could rise while JKM falls. Mitsubishi's Aethon gas acquisition (2.1 Bcf/d, about 15 Mtpa LNG-equivalent, 16 Jan 2026: https://www.acnnewswire.com/press-release/english/104689/mitsubishi-corporation-announces-acquisition-of-haynesville-shale-gas-business-in-louisiana-and-texas) is a partial hedge. (Agent could not confirm the close; Fortune, 14 Jul 2026, confirms it closed.)
- Severity: **Medium.**

**7. Shipping rate swings and route risk**
- Evidence:
  - Spark's Atlantic rate hit $278,250/day (4 Mar 2026: https://lngprime.com/?p=179245). It fell to $9k/day on 24 Aug, a record low for the season (https://globallnghub.com/lng-freight-rates-collapse-as-european-demand-grows/).
  - More than 400 carriers are on order, equal to 45–50% of the fleet, with 90–100 deliveries a year in 2026–27 (IEA Q3).
  - In April, 31 of 34 US cargoes to Asia sailed around the Cape. Platts sources say auctioned Panama Canal slots are impractical for spot cargoes (Platts, 5 May 2026: https://cyprusshippingnews.com/2026/05/05/us-lng-arbitrage-to-asia-open-via-panama-canal-laden-flows-limited/).
  - NOAA put the chance of El Niño, which threatens canal water levels, at 82% (21 May 2026: https://gcaptain.com/panama-canal-near-capacity-as-hormuz-crisis-drives-surge-in-u-s-energy-exports/).
  - A drone struck an LNG floating terminal at Damietta, Egypt on 29 Jul (https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/explosion-lng-terminal-egypt-damietta-port).
  - The Houthis reached the Bab al-Mandab strait in September (ACLED, 1 Oct 2026: https://acleddata.com/report/houthi-gains-around-bab-al-mandab-raise-risks-red-sea-shipping-and-global-oil-markets).
- Why it hurts DGE: it holds long-term charters, for example the 174,000 m³ DIAMOND GAS NEXUS for Cameron cargoes (26 Sep 2026: https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/new-lng-carrier-for-diamond-global-energy-named-diamond-gas-nexus/). Spare ships lose money when rates are slack, rate spikes punish a ship shortfall, and US Gulf-to-Asia voyages are long.
- Severity: **Medium-High.**

**8. LNG Canada: ramp-up done, but more volume and new rivals coming**
- Evidence:
  - Commissioning had setbacks in 2025: Train 1 received about 115 MMcf/d of feed gas against roughly 930 needed (21 Aug 2025: https://eastdaley.com/the-burner-tip/lng-canada-commissioning-continues-with-bumps).
  - Utilisation reached about 87% by April 2026 (8 Jul 2026: https://eastdaley.com/daley-note/canada-steps-into-breach-to-become-a-global-lng-player).
  - Phase 2 reached final investment decision on 28 Sep 2026, taking capacity from 14 to 28 Mtpa. Mitsubishi holds 15% and lifts its own share. Asia is about 10 days away, roughly half the Gulf Coast-via-Panama time (https://boereport.com/2026/09/29/lng-canada-announces-phase-2-final-investment-decision/).
  - Energía Costa Azul, on Mexico's Pacific coast, shipped its first cargo in July (9 Jul 2026: https://gcaptain.com/first-lng-cargo-ships-from-totalenergies-mexican-plant-energia-costa-azul/). Woodfibre is due in 2027 and Cedar in 2028 (East Daley).
- Why it hurts DGE: the short route to Asia is real but now shared. Mitsubishi's extra share is about 2 Mtpa (15% × 14 Mtpa), arriving in the early 2030s.
- Severity: **Medium** (Low in the near term).

**9. Force majeure chains, credit and customer checks**
- Evidence:
  - QatarEnergy extended its force majeure in September. Qatari exports were 1.6 Mt in September, under a quarter of pre-war levels, and about 0.6 Mt is expected for October (Reuters with Kpler data, 12 Oct 2026: https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/10/12/qatar-lng-exports-remain-severely-disrupted-despite-recovery-in-gulf-oil-shipments).
  - Force majeure notices are being passed down chains of linked contracts, and hardship claims are following (DLA Piper, 30 Apr 2026: https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/04/lng-disputes-emerge-after-disruption-at-ras-laffan-strait-of-hormuz).
  - Italy's Edison had 24 Qatari cargoes affected (3 Aug 2026: https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/03/second-tanker-with-qatari-lng-struck-in-hormuz-within-a-month-as-force-majeure-widens).
  - Bangladesh awarded cargoes to two US firms whose supply record could not be verified, and at least eight August cargoes arrived late (Daily Star, 15 Sep 2026).
  - Demand for trade credit insurance is growing 43% a year (Oliver Wyman, March 2025 — older: https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2025/mar/commodity-trading-report-2025.html).
- Why it hurts DGE: it sells delivered cargoes to financially stressed buyers. An outage at Cameron or LNG Canada would pass through the same contract chains.
- Severity: **Medium-High.**

**10. Regulation and sanctions compliance**
- Evidence:
  - EU methane rules: on 14 Oct 2026 the Commission proposed delaying, to 2028, the requirement that importers show EU-equivalent methane measuring and reporting. First proof would be due 31 May 2029. The proposal is not yet adopted (https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/14/eus-methane-rules-face-two-year-delay-sparking-environmental-backlash).
  - In its default scenario, a Wood Mackenzie study for the industry body IOGP finds 43% (114 bcm) of EU gas imports could be excluded, including most US and Canadian supply (March 2026, industry-funded: https://iogpeurope.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/EU-Methane-Emissions-Regulation-Study-Summary-Report.pdf).
  - The EU bans Russian LNG under short-term contracts from 25 Apr 2026 and under long-term contracts from 1 Jan 2027 (IEA Q1). US-origin gas is exempt from proving its origin in advance; Canadian gas is not (19 Feb 2026: https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/european-commission-exempts-six-countries-from-prior-authorisation-for-gas-imports-under-repowereu-regulation/).
  - The EU's revised market-conduct rules (REMIT II) make LNG trade reporting permanent and require non-EU firms to name an EU representative (May 2024: https://www.bakerbotts.com/thought-leadership/publications/2024/may/eu-remit-impact-on-non-eu-based-energy-companies).
  - Japan's METI and the certifier MiQ will verify methane emissions to the top OGMP 2.0 reporting level (Level 5) across LNG supply chains into Japan (undated, about 2025: https://miq.org/miq-enters-partnership-with-japan-ministry/).
  - Sanctioned Russian LNG reaches China through ship-to-ship transfers (IEA Q3).
- Why it hurts DGE: Cameron's feed gas comes from third parties, so methane and origin data per cargo is hard to get.
- Severity: **Medium-Low.** DGE sells mostly to Asia, but these rules limit selling to the EU and Japanese buyers will want the same data.

**11. Singapore hub: talent, cost and tax**
- Evidence:
  - Singapore hosts more than 60 LNG traders and handled US$150bn of LNG trade in 2023. It is also centralising power-sector gas buying in one state entity, which leaves fewer local buyers (18 Feb 2025: https://www.meti.gov.sg/Newsroom/Speeches/2025/02/Speech-by-MOS-Alvin-Tan-at-19th-LNGA-2025).
  - Sumitomo's energy arm is weighing a Singapore LNG desk (Bloomberg headline only, 8 Jan 2026: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-08/sumitomo-s-energy-arm-may-set-up-lng-trading-desk-in-singapore).
  - Recent Bangladesh tenders were won by BP Singapore, Vitol, TotalEnergies, Aramco Trading and Posco (Daily Star, 15 Sep 2026).
  - The Global Trader Programme tax incentive runs to 2031, but a 15% domestic top-up tax applies from FY2025 under the global minimum tax (EY, February 2026: https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/singapore-budget-2026-upcoming-incentive-refresh-in-a-pillar-two-world).
  - Cost per trader, excluding bonuses, has risen 25% since 2019 (Oliver Wyman, March 2025).
- Why it hurts DGE: it competes with majors and traders for the same small pool of LNG traders, schedulers and analysts, and the tax advantage is shrinking.
- Severity: **Medium.** No reliable 2026 salary data found.

**12. Fragmented data, an older trading system, and an AI gap**
- Evidence:
  - BCG (15 Jul 2026, https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-transforming-energy-trading) says that in LNG, contract terms, schedules, vessel status, approvals and risk positions sit in separate systems, and data quality is the main barrier to AI. It names uses such as checking whether a sales contract allows a cargo diversion, flagging demurrage, and explaining profit and loss.
  - McKinsey (9 Mar 2026, https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mckinsey-shorter-commodity-volatility-cycles-are-reshaping-trading-strategies-and-value-capture/) reports shorter volatility cycles. Agentic AI raised efficiency in support functions by 50–60%.
  - In a Molecule survey of 400+ firms, 64% say their ETRM system does not support all their processes (9 Oct 2025: https://molecule.io/blog/energy-commodities-trading-in-2025-what-400-companies-told-us-about-etrm-ctrm-modernization/).
  - Mitsubishi's LNG trading and risk system for MC Global Gas and Diamond Gas International went live in 2018 (https://publicissapient.com/news/Publicis-Sapient-MC-Global-Gas-Corporation-and-Diamond-Gas-International-Collaborate-to-Deliver-ETRM-platform-for-LNG-Business).
- Why it hurts DGE: a Hormuz-type shock requires same-day replanning of cargoes, force majeure notices, credit and freight. An eight-year-old system may not keep up.
- Severity: **Medium.** This capability decides how well DGE handles points 1–10.

### 2026 events that matter
- **22–26 Jan:** Winter Storm Fern — Henry Hub spot above $30, feed gas down 40%.
- **End of February:** war starts and Hormuz is effectively closed (IEA Q3).
- **4 Mar:** record freight rate of $278k/day.
- **18–19 Mar:** strikes on Ras Laffan take 12.8 Mtpa (about 17% of Qatar's capacity) offline, with 3–5 years to repair (GlobalData).
- **March–June:** US approves 13% more exports from Plaquemines; CP2 Phase 2, Delfin and Commonwealth reach investment decisions; Golden Pass ships its first cargo in late April.
- **8 Apr:** ceasefire. **25 Apr:** EU ban on Russian LNG under short-term contracts starts.
- **Mid-June:** US–Iran interim deal; JKM falls 12%.
- **July:** fighting resumes and Hormuz shuts again; LNG tankers are hit on 7 and about 31 July; Damietta strike on 29 July; Costa Azul's first cargo.
- **September:** Asian spot reaches $27; Houthis take the Bab al-Mandab area; China Gas signs a 20-year deal with Venture Global; China gives no tariff relief on US LNG; LNG Canada Phase 2 decision on 28 Sep.
- **14 Oct:** EU proposes the methane-rule delay; Taiwan announces it will take 30% of Alaska LNG's output, terms unclear (https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/14/alaska-lng-taiwan-korea/).

### Unknowns and weak evidence
- Hormuz after mid-October: the IEA assumed it would reopen in Q3, which has not happened. No IEA Q4-2026 report found.
- Forward prices: no JKM forward curve dated after July; the March curve predates the renewed fighting. Rabobank's view (TTF €72/MWh in Q4-26, falling to the mid-€40s in 2027) is from a search snippet only.
- DGE-specific data: nothing on its contract book, hedges, charter fleet or systems.
- Cameron LNG: in 2026 only planned maintenance found. The DOE snapshot shows no Cameron capacity under construction, so the expansion has no investment decision yet.
- LNG Canada: no performance data for the second half of 2026.
- Missing data: no credible 2026 Singapore talent-cost survey, no 2026 margin-call data, no named AI case studies at LNG desks.
- Snippet-only items: LNG Prime freight figures, the Bloomberg Sumitomo item, the US port-fee suspension on Chinese-built ships ending 9 Nov 2026, Japan's mandatory emissions trading from 1 Apr 2026.
- Unverified: Korean, Thai and Vietnamese pledges to buy US LNG; the date of the Ras Laffan strike (sources say 1 March or 18–19 March).
