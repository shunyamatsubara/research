DGE Field Guide

Learn how Diamond Global Energy makes money from LNG and what can hurt that money, so your monitoring system watches the right events.

Your mission

MCD3 is building a market and geopolitical monitoring system for DGE, to be followed by early risk-signal detection and credit risk management. As the engineer on the project, you need to know which events matter to DGE, and why. Every lesson ends with something your system could watch.

Lessons

  2. DGE’s Pressures in 2026 Next

  3. Who Might Not Pay Planned

How to use this course

  1. Read one lesson. Each takes 15 to 20 minutes.
  2. Answer its quiz without looking back. Struggling a little is how memory forms.
  3. Copy your result and paste it into the chat with Claude. Your misses decide what the next lesson repeats.
  4. Ask Claude any question, any time. Claude is your teacher for this course.
