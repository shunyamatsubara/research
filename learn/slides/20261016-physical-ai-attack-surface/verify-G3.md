# verify-G3: independent fact check of facts-G3.md

Checked 2026-10-16 (files edited 2026-10-17 session time). Every row G201 to G248 was checked against the cited source, which I opened myself.

Method notes:
- WebSearch was not available (the per-turn search budget was used up), so no fresh searches were run. Every check was a direct fetch of the cited URL or of a primary copy found by URL pattern.
- EUR-Lex pages return an empty challenge page (HTTP 202) to tools, but the EU Publications Office Cellar copy is readable: `https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>` with headers `Accept: application/xhtml+xml` and `Accept-Language: eng`. I used it for Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 (32023R1230), the consolidated Machinery Regulation as at 27.07.2026 (02023R1230-20260727), Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (32026R1744) and Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (32024R1689). This resolved several things that facts-G3.md listed as "could not find" (the omnibus text, the 2023/1230 corrigendum reference).
- PDFs (Commission summary list, RfU sheets, NIST SP 800-218A, ETSI EN 304 223, CISA joint guidance, Automotive SPICE PAM 4.0) were downloaded and converted with pdftotext, so those quotes are verbatim.
- BSI Knowledge, IEC Webstore, AI Act Service Desk, Commission pages, EP pages and Quality-One were read through the WebFetch extractor (short fragments).

## Observations that were not added to facts-G3.md (no new facts allowed)

- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 Art. 3(2) adds Art. 20(10) to the Machinery Regulation: until harmonised standards or common specifications are referenced under the Machinery Regulation for high-risk AI systems, high-risk AI systems within its scope that comply with harmonised standards referenced or common specifications adopted under Arts. 40 and 41 of the AI Act "shall be presumed to be in conformity with the essential health and safety requirements set out in Annex III". Recital 42 says the same in words.
- The omnibus also adds Art. 42(3) to the AI Act: high-risk AI systems within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (CRA) that meet the conditions of Art. 12(1) of that Regulation are deemed to comply with the cybersecurity requirements of Art. 15 of the AI Act. This bears on G246 (Art. 15 itself is unamended).
- Machinery Regulation Art. 18, third paragraph: a non-professional user who carries out a substantial modification for own use is not a manufacturer. Relevant to G213 only for consumer use.
- The "What I could not find" bullets on the text of Regulation 2026/1744 and on the 2023/1230 corrigendum are now resolved (see G204, G207).
