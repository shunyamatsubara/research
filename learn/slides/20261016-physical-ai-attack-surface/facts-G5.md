| G406 | In MirrorDrift's simulation, the advantage of optimised mirror placement over random placement largely disappears once the mirror's nearest distance to the route exceeds about 3 m; the attack assumes a known fixed route, and the real-world mirror was actuated by hand outdoors. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11364 (PDF Sec.
