# Facts C: boundaries (what existing controls and standards cover, and leave open)

Collected 2026-10-16; independently verified 2026-10-16 (see verify-C-controls.md). Angle C of the article "physical AI attack surface". Question: when a learned model decides what a robot arm, an AMR or a car does, which existing controls and standards can see each way in?

How to read the table
- Tags in the notes column say where the fact lands for a supplier: [factory], [product], [supply-chain], [general].
- "Inference" in the notes marks my own reading, which the article must not present as the source's claim.
- reliability: primary = the legal text, standard body page, agency report or paper itself. secondary = a catalogue entry or a page that only describes the source.
- Quotes are exact strings from the text I read. Where a fetch tool abbreviated a quote, the notes say so.
- Legal texts (AI Act, Machinery Regulation, CRA, UN R155/R156, Regulation 2022/1426, Regulation 2026/1744) were read as Official Journal XHTML from the EU Publications Office (http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>, fetched with Accept: application/xhtml+xml and Accept-Language: en).
