# Facts A (mechanism) - ADAS / automated-driving perception in tier-1 parts

Collected 2026-10-16. Sub-topic: how a production ADAS / AD system turns sensor readings into brake and steering commands; where learned models sit; how they are updated; whether production systems use end-to-end learned driving models.

Reading notes. "Primary" = the originator's own page, paper, patent or regulation text. Quotes in the notes column were checked against text I downloaded (curl plus pdftotext, or the page text) unless the row says "via fetch tool", which means the fetch tool's extract was the only copy I could get (the site blocked direct download). Sites I could not read directly: unece.org, iso.org, eur-lex.europa.eu (bot challenge), denso.com (Access Denied), autosar.org (503 / TLS error), tesla.com (403). See the last section. Tags in notes: [factory], [product], [supply-chain], [general]. Nearly all rows land in [product] or [supply-chain] because this sub-topic is the shipped part and its model lifecycle; the factory floor appears only through the R156 sensor re-calibration clause (A250).

## Pipeline sketch, sensor to actuator

Inputs and outputs in this table are generic descriptions I wrote from the cited rows; they are not quotes. "Rate" is given only where a source gives one. Bosch and Denso do not publish per-step rates for their camera, fusion, planning or control (see last section).

| 8 | Vehicle motion control | Classical software | Accepted request | Coordinates brakes, steering, powertrain, chassis | Actuator set-points | Not public | "control the vehicle movement in all six degrees of freedom by centrally controlling the brakes, steering, powertrain, and chassis" (A236) |
| 9 | Command path | Not learned; frame format and checks are fixed | Set-points | Serialised as CAN / CAN FD / Ethernet frames; receiving ECU checks checksum and plausibility; SecOC adds a MAC where deployed | Frames to ESP, iBooster, EPS | Classical CAN max 1 Mbit/s; CAN FD frames up to 64 data bytes (A242); example periodic messages every 10 ms (A243) | "There is no source identifier or authentication built into CAN packets." (A237) |
| 10 | Actuation | Classical, redundant | Brake and steering commands | ESP with iBooster for braking; electric power steering with a second independent loop | Brake pressure, steering torque | Not public | "If an error occurs in the electronics, a second, independent loop takes over operation of the steering." (A235) |
| 11 | Model lifecycle (outside the loop) | Learned parts change here | Fleet sensor data | Trigger-based upload, labelling, retraining in the cloud, OTA to the vehicle under R156 (EU/UN) or MIIT filing (China) | New model version in the ECU | Cloud iteration "once every five days" (XPENG, A227); software into test cars "several times a day" (A223) | "a new machine learning model is trained ... and is then deployed to vehicles as an update" (A252) |
