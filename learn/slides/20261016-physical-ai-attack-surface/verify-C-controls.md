# Verification C: controls and standards (facts-C-controls.md)

Checked 2026-10-16 by an independent pass. Every row C1 to C60 was checked against the cited source or a fresh copy of it. Legal texts were re-fetched as Official Journal XHTML from the EU Publications Office (http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>, with Accept: application/xhtml+xml and Accept-Language: en; without the language header the endpoint returns 400). Current consolidated texts were also read for UN R155 (CELEX 42025X0005), the AI Act (02024R1689-20260727) and the Machinery Regulation (02023R1230-20260529). NIST, ENISA, CISA, ISA, OSHA, ATLAS, Sigstore, arXiv and JSA sources were opened and the quoted strings located.

Not reachable or not read: unece.org (403, so the R156 01 series text was not read), iso.org (403), eur-lex.europa.eu HTML (empty challenge page; the corrigendum OJ L 169 itself was not opened, only its marker in the consolidated text), paywalled IEC 62443 clause text.
