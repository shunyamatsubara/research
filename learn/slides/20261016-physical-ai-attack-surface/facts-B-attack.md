# facts-B-attack: angle B, scale (numbers behind the attacks and the incidents)

Compiled 2026-10-16. Scope: real numbers (success rates, distances, costs, poison fractions, sample counts, time to attack) with the conditions behind them, plus incident and advisory counts for automotive suppliers.

How to read the table:
- Reliability: primary = the paper, vendor page or official register itself; secondary = a newspaper or magazine reporting it; derived = my arithmetic on primary rows (inputs given in notes).
- Notes carry the exact quote behind the number, the test conditions (lab or road, white-box or black-box, model and version), a landing tag ([factory], [product], [supply-chain], [general]) and "DIFFERS" where a number differs between or within sources.
- Method note: the shared web-search budget ran out early in the session, so I worked from direct URLs (arXiv, vendor and agency pages, official repositories). I downloaded each paper or page and read the passage quoted. Pages I could not read are listed at the end.

- Eykholt (B1, B3): 66.67%, 73.33%, 80%, 84.8%, 87.5% and 100% all appear, depending on pattern (poster or sticker), model (LISA-CNN or GTSRB-CNN) and test (stationary or drive-by). Quote the pair for the pattern you mean.
